Zixiao Wang (王子骁)

Hi there! I am Zixiao Wang, a third-year undergraduate student at Peking University, majoring in Artificial Intelligence in Zhi Class (an honors program directed by Prof. Baoquan Chen) at the School of Intelligence Science and Technology. Also, I am currently exchanging at School of Computer Science at Carnegie Mellon University.

My research focuses on Large Language Models, Optimization and Multimodal Learning. I am particularly interested in applying efficient and effective optimization algorithms for pretraining and RL post training of large language models, as well as analyzing the theoretical properties behind the training process. I'm also interested in exploring the potential of multimodal learning, especially in the context of video understanding and generation.

I am honored to be advised by Prof. Huishuai Zhang and Prof. Dongyan Zhao in Wangxuan Institute of Computer Technology. I am also very fortunate to be currently working closely with Prof. Jiawen Zhang from University of Wisconsin-Madison. Previously, I also had the privilege to work with Prof. Yang Liu.
